Privacy Policy for War Thunder Purchase Analyzer

Last updated: ${new Date().toLocaleDateString()}

1. Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or purchase information.

2. Data Processing

All processing occurs locally in your browser:

  • No data is sent to external servers
  • No persistent storage is used
  • All information remains on your device

3. Permissions Explanation

  • store.gaijin.net access - Required to analyze purchase history
  • activeTab - Allows refreshing stats from popup
